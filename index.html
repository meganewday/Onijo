About Our Firm

A Legacy of Legal Excellence

SA Lateef & Co is one of the most established law firms in the Oke-Ogun zone of Oyo State. Founded by S.A. Lateef — an erudite Senior Counsel, Notary Public, and Officer of the Supreme Court of Nigeria — the firm has served individuals, businesses, and institutions with distinction since its registration in 2008 (Reg. No. KW1503).

In 2019, SA Lateef built a permanent law office — the first purpose-built law firm in Saki and the entire Oke-Ogun zone — a milestone that reflects the firm's commitment to excellence, permanence, and professional standards of the highest order.
